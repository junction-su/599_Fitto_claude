Responsible AI
Ethics by design, not compliance.
Every product decision in Fitto was evaluated for its impact on vulnerable users.
🔍
Explainability
Every suggestion shows "Why this today?" — users always understand the recommendation logic. No black box decisions.
🛡️
Privacy First
No health data sold. On-device preference learning. GDPR and HIPAA compliant by design from day one.
💚
Anti-Shame Design
Skip = valid signal, not failure. No guilt streaks. Rest days explicitly acknowledged. Compassion is a product feature.
🎯
Bounded AI Scope
Fitto does not replace doctors or therapists. Clear escalation paths for medical concerns. AI recommends, humans decide.
