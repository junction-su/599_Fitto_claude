# Fitto AI Product Pitch

## Slide 1 — Title Slide


**Speaker note:** Open with energy. "How many of you have downloaded a fitness app and quit within a week?" Pause. "That's the problem we're solving."

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem
**Layout:** 1 bold stat, 3 bullets left-aligned, right side: simple emoji/icon row

```
THE PROBLEM

80% of people quit fitness apps within 30 days.

→  Workouts are built for people already in shape
→  No app adapts to how you feel *today*
→  Anxiety about "doing it wrong" kills motivation

The gap: fitness apps optimize for athletes,
not for people just trying to start.
```

**Speaker note:** Ground this in a real feeling. Most fitness app failures are emotional, not physical — the app makes people feel inadequate.

## Slide 4 — Why Existing Apps Fail
**Layout:** 2-column comparison table (Them vs. Fitto)

```
WHY EXISTING APPS FAIL

  Existing Apps              Fitto
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  Static workout plans   →   Daily adaptation
  One-size-fits-all      →   Energy-level aware
  Shame-based streaks    →   Compassionate logging
  Ignores bad days       →   Skip = valid data
  No context memory      →   AI learns over time

The problem isn't lack of fitness content.
It's lack of personalization at the moment of action.
```

**Speaker note:** Emphasize the philosophical difference: Fitto treats a skip as useful signal, not failure.

## Slide 6 — Product Overview
**Layout:** Phone mockup center, 4 callout labels pointing to UI zones

```
MEET FITTO

  1. How's your energy today?    [Low / Medium / High]
  2. AI suggests a tailored workout
  3. Tap to start — timer begins
  4. Done or Skip — both logged
  5. Next session adapts

Two modes:
  💪 Fitto Classic    — character companion, gamified streaks
  🌿 Fitto Gentle     — anxiety-first, breath-first, judgment-free

[See Lovable prototype → full onboarding flow]
[See Claude prototype  → AI recommendation engine live]
```

**Speaker note:** Show screenshots from both prototypes here. The point: same AI, two UX philosophies.
